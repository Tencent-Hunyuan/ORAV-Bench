MEASURED AUDIO ENERGY TIMELINES (not perceptual scores)
These uniform-window RMS values are derived from each supplied source audio stream,
with no silence trimming, gain change, channel downmix or sample-rate change. Each
bin starts at start_s + index * window_s on that MEDIA's local playback timeline;
the final bin can be shorter. dbFS is 20*log10(RMS), floored at -100 only for display.
Stereo/channel energies are averaged, not phase-cancelling mixed waveforms.
Use this auxiliary signal to locate energy changes in time, checking actual audio
and video. It does not identify speech, event meaning, distortion, intent or quality.
High/low energy is not itself a defect or a synchronization verdict; speech energy
alone cannot establish lip sync. Do not infer any candidate must be different.
Missing/unavailable measurements do not mean silence. Keep uncertain semantic or
temporal correspondence uncertain. Actual audio remains supplied unchanged.
